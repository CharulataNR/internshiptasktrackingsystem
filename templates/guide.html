Roles and access

Administrator manages everything: interns, teams, tasks, assessments, users and settings.

Team Lead sees only their own team. A team lead can create and edit tasks for interns in that team, update task status, record assessments and download reports for that team. Interns, teams and users are managed by the administrator.

How to add interns

  1. Open Intern Master and click Add intern.
  2. Fill in name, DIT ID, email, 10-digit phone number, college, role and joining date. Fields marked * are mandatory.
  3. Optionally pick a team. Click Add intern.

The DIT ID must be unique; the system rejects duplicates. Use the search box (name, DIT ID or email) and the college/team filters to find interns. Use the pencil icon to edit and the bin icon to delete (this also deletes the intern's tasks).

How to create teams

  1. Open Team Management and click Create team.
  2. Enter a unique team name, a short description and, optionally, the team leader.
  3. On the team page use Manage members to add unassigned interns, remove members or change the team leader.

An intern belongs to one team at a time. To move an intern, edit the intern and choose the new team. The team page and the team list show the completion percentage of each team.

How to assign tasks

  1. Open Task Tracker and click New task (or Assign task on an intern's page).
  2. Enter the title and description, choose the intern (the DIT ID appears automatically) and set the assigned and due dates.
  3. Click Create task. A Task ID such as TSK-0042 is generated.

The due date cannot be earlier than the assigned date.

How to update task status

In the Task Tracker, change the status in the dropdown on the task's row; it saves immediately. You can also open a task and use Update status, or edit the task to change dates, remarks or assessment details.

Typical flow: Not Started → In Progress → Submitted → Under Review → Completed (or Rejected for rework). The submission date is filled in automatically when a task is submitted.

How to track overdue tasks

A task that is Not Started or In Progress after its due date is flagged Overdue automatically; nothing needs to be updated by hand.

See the Overdue card and the overdue table on the Dashboard, or filter the Task Tracker by status Overdue. The "Overdue Tasks" Excel report lists them with the number of days late.

How to assess submissions

Open Assessment Management, click Assess on a submitted task, choose the assessment status, enter a score from 0 to 100 and optional remarks. Approved needs a score and completes the task; Rejected sends it back for rework. Every assessment is kept in the task's history.

How to generate reports

Open Reports. The table shows every team's progress; click Open report for a printable team lead report (use Print / save as PDF in your browser).

How to export Excel files

On the Reports page, click Download .xlsx on any of the eight report cards: Intern Master, Team Allocation, Complete Task Tracker, Completed Tasks, Pending Tasks, Overdue Tasks, Assessment Report and Team Lead Report. You can also filter the Task Tracker first and use Export filtered Excel to download exactly what you see. Files include headings, filters on every column, frozen header rows and colour-coded statuses.

Tips and rules

  • Pending on the dashboard means Not Started; the "Pending Tasks" Excel report lists all open work (every task that is not yet Completed).
  • Phone numbers are validated as 10-digit Indian mobile numbers (an optional +91 is accepted).
  • Change your password under Settings. Sign out when you finish.
